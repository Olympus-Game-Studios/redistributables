# The Devil You Know
### Design Document (Literal Draft Based Only on Provided Notes)

## 1. Summary

*The Devil You Know* is a point and click dating simulator.  
The player controls **Jack**, a man who has just moved into the city of Whitechapel in 2025.

Jack finds an **old school doctor’s bag** in his room’s closet. He becomes interested in it and starts using the bag in his daily work as a doctor. He uses it partly for the niche and partly because he feels drawn to it by an unseen force.

Jack begins going out in the city and meeting different people, forming relationships. These people include fellow doctors and nurses, random citizens, and law enforcement. The early objective of the game is to build a relationship with one of these characters.

After the first couple of interactions, people Jack dates begin to disappear. This happens coincidentally right after Jack sleeps with them. The police begin questioning Jack. During these investigations the player character experiences flashbacks and blurry visions of violent screams and actions being taken against the people he has dated.

The central mystery is that the killings are connected to the doctor’s bag. The bag is a haunted item that contains the spirit of Jack the Ripper. The influence of the bag pushes the player character toward becoming a modern version of Jack the Ripper unless the player chooses to turn themselves in or destroy the bag.

## 2. Main Goals

### Goal Path 1
Help the police catch the killer.  
In the end the killer is revealed to be the player.  
The player can choose to:
- Run away  
- Turn themselves in  

### Goal Path 2
Unravel the mystery through clues hidden:
- Around Jack’s room  
- In the outside world  

This path reveals the truth about the doctor’s bag and Jack the Ripper’s spirit.  
The player can choose to:
- Embrace the fact they are becoming the reincarnated Jack the Ripper  
- Turn themselves in  

### Goal Path 3
Unravel the mystery but do not turn yourself in.  
The player tries to deal with the revelation on their own.

### Optional Ending Choice
Destroy the bag.  
This ends the game immediately, but a new person moves into the room afterward and finds the “destroyed” bag in the same place.  
This shows that the cycle of violence continues.

In all endings the bag eventually finds someone new to continue the dark legacy.

## 3. Police Investigation

The police investigate the disappearances throughout the game.  
Depending on the player’s actions, the secret may be revealed earlier than expected.

The police characters question Jack at different stages.  
If suspicion becomes too high, the player may face confrontation or arrest sooner than intended.

## 4. Characters

### Jack (Player Character)
A doctor who just moved to Whitechapel. Finds the haunted doctor’s bag.

### Dr. Ruckus
A doctor from Jack’s workplace.

### Fred
Jack’s nosy neighbor.

### Officer Brent
A police officer whose patrol area includes Jack’s neighborhood.

### Detective Lance
A homicide detective.  
He can either:
- Arrest Jack  
- Work with Jack to uncover the mystery  
He can also be killed if the player goes down the bad route.

### Sarah
A local barista who Jack can date. A potential victim.

### Anna, Julie, and Rose
A group of friends who do yoga in the park.

### Robert (Bob)
Owner of the liquor store.

### Jeff
Jack’s landlord.
